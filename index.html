What is Recapp?

Recapp is a first-of-its-kind AI sports media platform built by leaders in sports and artificial intelligence, backed by top venture capitalists.

We’re obsessed with giving fans the fastest, cleanest way to see the plays that matter. We combine real-time data, AI, and human curation to turn every game into a feed of can’t-miss moments.

As a Content Associate, you sit at the center of that experience—pairing your sports IQ with strong writing and a sharp editorial eye.

What you’ll do as a Sports Content Associate

You’ll work directly with our Head of Content to ensure every highlight, headline, and story on Recapp feels accurate, on-brand, and worth a fan’s time.

Shape the voice of Recapp

  • Review and refine copy for grammar, accuracy, and tone
  • Enforce and evolve style guidelines across leagues and sports
  • Help ensure every highlight feels sharp, fun, and on-brand

Own quality across the app

  • Participate in QA operations across the platform
  • Identify and escalate content issues you spot in the product
  • Help build and refine curated sections of the app

Create social-ready sports content

  • Bolster in-app stories with smart social curation
  • Bring knowledge of the wider sports media landscape
  • Create content for platforms like Instagram, TikTok, X, and more
You’ll love this role if you… Live on sports Twitter/TikTok, write clean copy, and care about the details.
